1. Program description

Field OS Alerts is an SMS alert program operated by Anthony Albert, a sole proprietor operating a personal field operations and quality assurance business under his own legal name. It sends sole proprietor personal business communication to one recipient only: the enrolled recipient, Anthony Albert himself, the account owner and sole proprietor. No customers, no employees, and no third parties receive these messages. Messages notify the recipient when a job needs attention — for example, when a crew's photo uploads stop for 30 minutes or a required before-video is missing — and include night report summaries of jobs tracked and jobs needing follow-up.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on job activity. The recipient may receive multiple alerts per night when jobs need attention, and fewer on nights with no issues.
