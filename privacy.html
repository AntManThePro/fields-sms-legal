1. Information we collect

We collect the mobile phone number you use to opt in to Field OS Alerts, and the fact that you sent START to (737) 214-9344. We do not collect any other personal information through this SMS program.

2. How we use it

Your phone number is used only to send you Field OS Alerts — internal field operations and QA job alerts and night report summaries — and to process HELP and STOP requests.

3. SMS information is not shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS opt-in information with any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Opt-out

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to (737) 214-9344. After you opt out, your number will no longer receive Field OS Alerts, and your opt-in record will be retained only as required to honor the opt-out.

5. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your phone number from unauthorized access.
